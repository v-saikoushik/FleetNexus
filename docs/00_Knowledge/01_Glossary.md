\# FleetNexus – Logistics Glossary

\*\*Document Version:\*\* 1.0.0

\*\*Status:\*\* Active

\*\*Last Updated:\*\* 13 July 2026

\*\*Owner:\*\* Koushik

\---

\# Purpose

This glossary defines the logistics, transport, and business terminology used throughout the FleetNexus documentation.

It serves as a common reference for developers, designers, business analysts, testers, and future contributors.

\---

\# A

\## Advance Payment

An amount paid before a trip begins. This may be paid to the fleet owner or directly to the driver for trip-related expenses such as food, fuel, or tolls.

\---

\# B

\## Bilty (Lorry Receipt / LR)

A transport document issued after goods are accepted for transportation. It contains shipment details and acts as proof that the transporter has received the goods.

\---

\# C

\## Consignor

The person or organization sending the goods.

Example:

A cement factory shipping products.

\---

\## Consignee

The person or organization receiving the goods.

Example:

A warehouse or dealer receiving the shipment.

\---

\# D

\## Detention

Additional charges applied when a truck is forced to wait longer than the agreed loading or unloading time.

\---

\## Dispatch

The process of assigning a truck and sending it to collect goods from the pickup location.

\---

\# E

\## E-Way Bill

An electronic document required under GST regulations for transporting goods above specified limits within India.

\---

\# F

\## Factory Transport Manager

A user responsible for planning transport requirements, creating load requests, and tracking deliveries.

\---

\## Fleet Owner

A person or company that owns one or more transport vehicles and manages drivers, trips, maintenance, and expenses.

\---

\# G

\## Gross Weight

The total weight of the goods including packaging.

\---

\# L

\## Load

A transportation requirement created by a factory or customer.

A load includes:

\- Material

\- Weight

\- Pickup

\- Destination

\- Number of Trucks

\- Rate

\- Schedule

\---

\## Loading

The process of placing goods onto the assigned vehicle.

\---

\## Loading Slip

A document generated during loading that records material, quantity, vehicle information, and dispatch details.

\---

\# P

\## POD (Proof of Delivery)

A document signed after successful unloading confirming that the goods were delivered correctly.

\---

\# Q

\## Queue / Union Series

The ordered sequence maintained by a lorry union to allocate transport jobs fairly among registered members.

\---

\# R

\## Route

The path followed by a vehicle from pickup to destination.

\---

\# T

\## Toll Receipt

Proof of payment collected while crossing toll plazas during transportation.

\---

\## Trip

A complete transportation operation beginning with truck assignment and ending after delivery, payment settlement, and trip closure.

\---

\## Trip Settlement

The financial reconciliation performed after a trip, including freight, fuel, tolls, advances, repairs, penalties, and final profit.

\---

\# U

\## Unloading

The process of removing transported goods from the vehicle at the destination.

\---

\## Union

A local transport association responsible for coordinating transport requests and allocating work among registered fleet owners.

\---

\# V

\## Vehicle Profile

A permanent digital record containing all information related to a truck, including documents, trips, maintenance history, expenses, and ownership details.

\---

\# Future Updates

This glossary will continue to expand as FleetNexus introduces additional logistics concepts, financial terminology, AI modules, GPS integrations, and transport workflows.
