\# FleetNexus – Product Roadmap

\*\*Document Version:\*\* 1.0.0

\*\*Status:\*\* Active

\*\*Last Updated:\*\* 16 July 2026

\*\*Owner:\*\* Koushik

\---

\# Purpose

This roadmap defines the planned development journey of FleetNexus from product planning to a production-ready Transport ERP.

The roadmap is intended to guide development while remaining flexible enough to accommodate new business requirements and user feedback.

\---

\# Phase 1 – Product Foundation ✅

Completed

Deliverables

\- Project Vision

\- Product Principles

\- Product Idea

\- Knowledge Base

\- Business Concepts

\- Technology Stack

\- MVP Definition

Goal

Establish a strong understanding of the logistics domain and define the overall product vision.

\---

\# Phase 2 – Core Platform

Objective

Build the foundational platform required by every user.

Modules

\- Authentication

\- User Management

\- Organization Management

\- Role-Based Access Control

Milestone

Users can securely access the system based on their role.

\---

\# Phase 3 – Fleet Operations

Objective

Digitize daily fleet operations.

Modules

\- Fleet Management

\- Driver Management

\- Trip Management

\- Load Management

\- Union Management

\- Expense Management

\- Payment Management

Milestone

A transport company can manage its complete daily operations digitally.

\---

\# Phase 4 – Documents \& Automation

Objective

Reduce paperwork and manual data entry.

Modules

\- Document Management

\- OCR Receipt Scanner

\- Vehicle Documents

\- Driver Documents

\- POD Management

Milestone

Most operational documents become digital and searchable.

\---

\# Phase 5 – Smart Features

Objective

Introduce intelligent automation.

Modules

\- AI Assistant

\- GPS Tracking

\- WhatsApp Integration

\- FASTag Integration

\- Notifications

\- Analytics Dashboard

Milestone

FleetNexus becomes an intelligent transport management platform.

\---

\# Phase 6 – Enterprise Expansion

Future enhancements may include:

\- Predictive Maintenance

\- Route Optimization

\- Fuel Theft Detection

\- IoT Integration

\- Voice Assistant

\- Multi-language Support

\- Enterprise ERP Integrations

\---

\# Development Philosophy

Every phase should produce a working and testable system.

Development should prioritize:

\- Stability

\- Scalability

\- Security

\- User Experience

\- Business Value

Features will only be added when they contribute meaningful value to transport operations.

\---

\# Success Criteria

FleetNexus will be considered successful when:

\- Core transport workflows are fully digital.

\- Multiple stakeholders can collaborate within one platform.

\- Operational paperwork is significantly reduced.

\- Business decisions are supported by centralized data and automation.

\- The platform remains easy to use and scalable.
